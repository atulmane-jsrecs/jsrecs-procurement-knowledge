JSR Engineering & Consultancy Services (JSRECS) is an India-based merchant exporter and strategic sourcing organisation. SSBB EXIM™ is the international export, sourcing and procurement brand/division of JSRECS.

Depending on buyer requirement, specification, quantity, destination, compliance requirements and commercial conditions, JSRECS may identify, evaluate and source from suitable Indian manufacturers, processors, authorised suppliers and other qualified supply partners.

Core Procurement Capabilities

Strategic Sourcing

Indian source discovery and structured procurement support based on buyer requirements.

Manufacturer Discovery

Identification and evaluation of potential Indian manufacturers and qualified supply partners.

Supplier Qualification

Review of corporate identity, capability, specifications, capacity, quality evidence and commercial fit.

Export Execution

Commercial and documentation coordination through the international supply process.

Knowledge & Qualification Framework

Manufacturer Qualification

How international buyers can evaluate an Indian manufacturer before importing.

Supplier Verification

Identity, manufacturing claims, product capability, technical evidence, capacity and documentation.

Technical Evaluation

Specification, CoA/testing evidence, samples, inspection and product-fit considerations.

Commercial Evaluation

Quantity, destination, packing, Incoterm, delivery schedule and export feasibility.

Evidence-led by design

Public information in this network is intended to distinguish manufacturer evidence, supplier or sourcing relationships, product evidence, CoA/testing evidence, sourcing status and export capability. Commercially important claims should be supported by appropriate documentation.

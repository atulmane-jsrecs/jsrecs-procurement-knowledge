How to Qualify an Indian Manufacturer

Manufacturer qualification should connect a buyer's requirement with evidence about the supplier's capability, product fit, quality and commercial feasibility.

REQUIREMENT → QUALIFY → SOURCE → VERIFY → PRICE → QUOTE → NEGOTIATE → ORDER → EXECUTE → REPEAT

SSBB EXIM™ is the international export, sourcing and procurement brand/division of JSR Engineering & Consultancy Services (JSRECS), India.

Qualification Checklist

  • Identity and business information
  • Relevant manufacturing capability
  • Product specifications
  • Capacity
  • Quality records and CoA where applicable
  • Samples or inspection where required
  • Packaging and logistics
  • Export documentation
  • Commercial feasibility

Evidence First

Use the evidence appropriate to the product and transaction. Avoid treating a general catalogue statement as proof of a specific batch or shipment.

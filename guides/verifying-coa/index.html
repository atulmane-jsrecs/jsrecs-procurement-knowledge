How to Verify an Indian Supplier's Certificate of Analysis (CoA)

A CoA should be reviewed as batch-specific evidence and checked against the buyer's specification and the supplier documentation supporting that batch.

REQUIREMENT → QUALIFY → SOURCE → VERIFY → PRICE → QUOTE → NEGOTIATE → ORDER → EXECUTE → REPEAT

SSBB EXIM™ is the international export, sourcing and procurement brand/division of JSR Engineering & Consultancy Services (JSRECS), India.

Check the Batch Identity

Review batch or lot reference, date, product description and issuing entity.

Check Parameters

Compare reported parameters with the buyer's required limits and test methods where provided.

Check Consistency

Confirm that the CoA refers to the product, source and batch being offered. Do not substitute one batch reference for another.

Independent Testing

Where required by the buyer or transaction, arrange appropriate sampling, inspection or laboratory testing.

How to Compare Indian Suppliers

Supplier comparison should use the same buyer-defined criteria for each candidate source so technical and commercial differences are visible.

REQUIREMENT → QUALIFY → SOURCE → VERIFY → PRICE → QUOTE → NEGOTIATE → ORDER → EXECUTE → REPEAT

SSBB EXIM™ is the international export, sourcing and procurement brand/division of JSR Engineering & Consultancy Services (JSRECS), India.

Comparison Framework

  • Product specification
  • Quality evidence
  • Capacity and lead time
  • Packaging
  • Inspection/testing
  • Price basis
  • Inland logistics
  • Export documentation
  • Destination compliance

Decision Record

The comparison should record evidence, assumptions and items requiring confirmation before order placement.
